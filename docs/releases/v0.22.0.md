# v0.22.0

v0.22.0 is the release in which a sender can withdraw a message it posted,
with a short note put in its place. It begins on the relay, which keeps
beside every message posted to it what a withdrawal is authorised by and
takes a withdrawal on a route of its own. The relay's schema rises from
13 to 14, and its wire level from 2 to 3. Nothing a v0.21.1 client sends
or reads changes, so a v0.21.1 client and a v0.22.0 relay work together
as they did.

**Going back to v0.21.1.** A relay's database carried to schema 14 goes
back only by the copy the upgrade kept beside it, `relay.db` with
`.before-schema-13` appended, with v0.21.1's binary. That costs what
going back by the copy always costs, which [`docs/relay.md`](../relay.md)
section 1.1 sets out: every message that arrived after the upgrade, and
every change made on the relay since. A v0.21.1 relay refuses a database
at schema 14 by name, as it refuses any from a newer release. Nothing on
a client's machine changes.

## What changed

**The relay keeps a withdrawal key's stored form beside each message.**
A message's key is derived from its sealed bytes, never minted: SHA-256
over the domain `ccx-withdraw-v1`, the recipient's fingerprint and the
ciphertext, each length-prefixed, and over a stream's first 4,096 bytes
in place of the ciphertext. The sender can compute it from the copy it
sealed, and so can the relay from the copy it stores. The relay keeps
only a hash of the key, in a new column of the `messages` table, written
for a message posted to the relay and for a stream placed there. A knock,
a message delivered by another relay's courier and every message stored
before the upgrade keep the empty string, and are not withdrawable. The
column names nobody and goes with its row. No answer carries it and no
log line carries it.

**A relay takes a withdrawal, and says so as wire level 3.** `POST
/v1/mailbox/{fp}/withdraw/{id}` carries the key and the note's envelope,
and the relay puts the note in the message's place in one transaction
when the stored form of the key matches the row's. The route is a post in
every respect but one: it is charged to the sender's post budgets, held to
the allow list and to the note's channel, and stored once by its note, as
a post is by its envelope, so a withdrawal asked again is answered as the
first. The one exception is the channel's period: a message posted while
its channel was open can be withdrawn after it closes, the note on that
channel and no other, and a note stored while the channel is closed
cannot itself be withdrawn. Every row the key does not open is the one `404`,
with the new code `not-withdrawable`. [`docs/relay.md`](../relay.md)
section 1.11 gives the order of the checks, and
[`docs/security.md`](../security.md) what the route tells and to whom.
